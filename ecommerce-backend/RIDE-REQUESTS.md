# Daily ride requests

Run `npm run migrate:ride-requests` after deploying backend code. This adds `Users.needsCoach` (default true), `Coaches.dailyRate` (nullable), the separate `RideRequests` table and a `RidePricings` row for the flat gear daily rate. It preserves existing bookings, customers and coach hourly rates. Run `npm run seed:ride-pricing` to populate starter prices where none exist: gear $50/rider/day, Alex $150/day, Jake $250, Marcus $225, Liam $300, Ethan $175, Owen $225 and Noah $275. Existing non-null rates are preserved. Rates are editable under Bookings → Daily rental rates, or individually through the coach editor; hourly rates are never converted automatically.

The public `/booking` flow is now: the customer?s selected bike and inclusive consecutive rental dates (minimum two days), a separate optional buddies step, optional track, an independent Yes/No extra-gear choice for every rider (Yes reveals the item picker; No clears gear), one group coach, review and request. Step one shows only the customer?s bike and its calendar, with a Change bike selector. Buddy bike choices are filtered by the full date range and remaining group capacity. Rental dates come from bike inventory and schedules, independently of track dates. Buddies require a name, email and phone but do not need accounts. Customers sign in first. Admins alone may edit their `needsCoach` radio preference under Customers; customers see it read-only on their profile. A false preference makes coaching optional, while true requires a coach at quote, request and approval time.

Only this month and next month in Pacific time can be booked. Track selection may be skipped (`trackId: null`); estimates store `track: null` and approval does not require a track. All tracks are shown regardless of rental dates. Track calendars do not restrict quoting, request submission or approval; a selected track only needs to exist. Equipment/coach schedules apply by day: an explicitly closed day is excluded; a day with any opening hours is eligible for daily rental. Missing equipment calendars retain the existing default of open. Remaining stock is checked across every selected day, aggregated over all riders and gear. Confirmed bookings and existing pending payment holds count as occupied. Pending ride requests do not occupy inventory.

`GET /ride-requests/catalog?startDate=YYYY-MM-DD&endDate=YYYY-MM-DD` returns filtered daily products, coaches, all tracks and `gearDailyAmount` in cents. Without dates it returns bike choices, their available dates, all tracks and the allowed range. The `bikes` list always includes bike date availability across the full two-month window; `products` is filtered to the selected range when supplied. Bikes/coaches require daily rates. Gear items do not need individual prices: each rider may choose at most one item per category and pays exactly one flat gear charge per day if any gear is selected, or zero if none is selected. Individual gear items remain in estimates as included lines for inventory and sizing; a single `gearPackage` line carries the charge. All amounts use integer cents and inclusive day counts; one coach is charged once per group per day. Nothing is charged per ride. No track admission fee is invented or added.

`GET /ride-requests/pricing` and `PUT /ride-requests/pricing` are admin-only. Submit `{ gearDailyRate: "50.00", coaches: [{ id: "UUID", dailyRate: "150.00" }] }`. Blank/null rates disable the respective rental option. Rate changes are atomic and rechecked at request submission and approval; previously stored estimates retain their historical itemization.

Authenticated customers use `POST /ride-requests/quote`, `POST /ride-requests/` and `GET /ride-requests/mine`. Submit the draft plus `clientKey` (UUID), `expectedTotalAmount` and `privacyAccepted: true`. Repeated keys return the existing request. Requests record consent time and policy version. Buddy details are stored in the request, visible only to its owner and authorized staff. No messages are automatically sent to buddies.

Staff with `booking:view` review requests on Bookings. `PATCH /ride-requests/admin/:id` requires `booking:update` and accepts `approved`, `rejected` or `cancelled` with an optional customer-visible note. Approval locks the request, track, customer, sorted products and coach, revalidates dates, coaching requirement, prices and inventory, then creates one confirmed full-day booking allocation per rider per day with linked gear. These internal allocations share the existing inventory system, remain unpaid, carry `meta.source = ride-request` and do not duplicate the request in customer or legacy admin booking lists. Repeat approval does not create extra allocations. Cancellation releases all allocations. Price changes require a fresh customer request.

Requests and approvals do not initiate Stripe checkout. The legacy checkout-creation endpoint returns 410; already-created Stripe sessions and historical bookings retain their existing reconciliation paths. Payment is arranged separately after approval; request estimates do not represent collected revenue.

Validate with `node --test tests/ride-request-validation.test.cjs tests/track-availability.test.cjs` and `npm run verify:ride-requests`. The integration verifier creates isolated temporary records, checks concurrent approval and cancellation, and removes its own records afterward. It does not send email or process payments.
